# Strategic Recommendations and Operational Roadmap

## Priority 1: Delivery Estimation Dynamic Recalibration and Regional Hubs
- **Problem:** Cross-state orders take 14.7 days vs. 7.5 days for same-state, with North/Northeast late rates exceeding 18-21%. Olist systematically over-promises delivery estimates in these regions.
- **Action 1:** Recalibrate dynamic estimated delivery dates (EDT) based on origin-destination pairs, adding realistic buffer days for interstate transit to eliminate late delivery penalties.
- **Action 2:** Establish regional 3PL fulfillment partnerships in Recife (PE) and Salvador (BA) to provide localized fulfillment for the underserved Northeast.

---

## Priority 2: Multi-Seller Experience and Tracking Unification
- **Problem:** Split-seller orders have a 6.47x higher odds ratio for bad reviews (46% low-review rate on-time) due to disjointed notifications and staggered arrivals.
- **Action 1:** Implement unified order tracking combining separate carrier tracking tokens into a single multi-package customer dashboard.
- **Action 2:** Proactively message customers post-purchase: 'Your items are arriving in 2 separate shipments' with individual timeline trackers.

---

## Priority 3: Heavy-Goods Packaging and Dedicated Carrier Routing
- **Problem:** Bulky furniture categories (Office Furniture: 3.51 mean rating) underperform due to handling and transit damage.
- **Action 1:** Enforce packaging audits and drop-test standards for heavy and fragile goods sellers.
- **Action 2:** Route furniture shipments through specialized freight carriers rather than standard parcel couriers.

---

## Action Guidance: Deprecate Payment Overhauls
- **Empirical Proof:** Odds ratio for payment installments is 1.02x, and payment types show comparable satisfaction.
- **Resource Reallocation:** Reallocate developer and product management capacity away from checkout payment alterations and direct it toward delivery tracking and logistics coordination.
